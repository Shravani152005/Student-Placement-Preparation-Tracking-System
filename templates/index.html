Practice multiple-choice questions across Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability. Features automatic scoring, countdown timer, and comprehensive step-by-step solutions.

Log problems solved across LeetCode, HackerRank, and GeeksforGeeks. Tag by programming language, difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), and status (Solved, In Progress, Need Revision).

Job Application Pipeline

Track companies and roles applied for. Organize by stages: Applied, Shortlisted, Technical Interview, Selected, and Rejected. Record CTC packages, links, and interview notes.

Master top campus placement interview questions categorized by Java, Python, SQL, OOPs, and HR. Includes STAR method answers and interviewer tips with progress tracking.
